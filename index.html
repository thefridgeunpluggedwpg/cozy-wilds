Cozy Wilds

a gentle wilderness survival RPG

No hunger or thirst timers, no rush. Boiled water, toasted berries, a warm fire and a good night's sleep give gentle buffs. Gather 🪵 wood from trees, fill your 🫙 jug at the lake, stream or well, and boil it on the fire. Build a lean-to anywhere, then work up the shelter ladder: tarp camp, the old trapper shack or the camper van, then fortify or upgrade it. Wolves, coyotes, bears and raccoons only prowl at night: they keep away from firelight, and sleeping in a shelter is always safe. They do like to ransack empty camps, so expect a little repair work.

Desktop: WASD/arrows or click to walk · E/Space act · B build · F feed fire · Z sleep · wheel zoom · H hide UI · Esc pause.
Touch: left thumb joystick · Ⓐ act · 🔨 build · 🪵 feed · 💤 sleep · pinch to zoom · two fingers to pan.
